Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 14 September 2026

No refunds on credit purchases

Credit packs are a one-time digital purchase and are delivered to your account immediately after payment. All credit purchases are final and non-refundable, including for credits you have not used.

Every account gets 5 free job board searches, with free AI scoring, so you can try the service before paying.

Cancellation

There is no subscription, so there is nothing to cancel and you will never be charged again automatically. You can stop using the service at any time, or ask us to close your account. Closing your account also removes any unused credits.

Failed, duplicate or undelivered payments

The no-refund rule does not apply to payment errors. If:

  • money was debited but the payment failed, or
  • you were charged twice for the same purchase, or
  • your payment succeeded but the credits did not reach your account within 24 hours,

email banerjeesoumyadip1991@gmail.com within 7 days with your registered email and the Razorpay payment ID. We will either add the missing credits or refund the extra charge to your original payment method. Refunds are processed within 5–7 working days of approval; your bank may take longer to show them.

A failed payment that was debited is usually reversed automatically by your bank or Razorpay within 5–7 working days.

Actions that failed

If a search or AI request fails on our side, the app does not charge credits for it.
