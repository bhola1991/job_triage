Three rules that keep it honest
A row carries at most one accent. If a job is both closing and overdue, the nearer deadline wins and the other becomes plain text.
Score is never an accent. A 72 and a 41 are both just figures — colour says what to do, the number says how good it is.
Dark states the accent on the edge; light states it across the whole cell. Same instruction, read the way each ground reads best.
