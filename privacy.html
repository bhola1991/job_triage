Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

Job Triage is owned and operated by Soumyadip Banerjee, Kolkata, India. This policy explains what we collect and what happens to it.

What we store

You can create an account with a username and password, or by signing in with Google. If you use Google, your name and email address come from your Google account, and Google's own privacy policy applies to that sign-in. Either way, the following is stored in a database hosted by Supabase:

  • your email address and username;
  • the text of any CV you paste or upload, and the profile and career directions derived from it;
  • every job, score, note, contact and event you record;
  • your credit balance, free searches remaining, and a record of your purchases (order ID, pack, amount, payment ID).

Each account can read only its own data. This is enforced by the database itself (row-level security), not just by the app.

Payments

Payments are handled by Razorpay. Your card, UPI or bank details go directly to Razorpay and are never seen or stored by us. Razorpay's own privacy policy applies to that information.

Services that process your data

To search and score jobs, the app sends the minimum needed to these providers:

  • DeepSeek, to build your profile, score jobs and draft messages. When you first add a CV, the whole text of it is sent so the profile can be extracted from it. After that, each scoring request carries the job text plus your name, location and country, your level and years of experience, your headline, strengths, gaps and what is unusual about you, and the titles of the direction being searched;
  • TypeSafe (their System One model, “Jev”), which decides the flags shown on a job — whether the posting is too senior, gated behind a credential, or too thin to judge. It receives one posting at a time (title, company, location and up to 4,000 characters of the description) together with your name, location, level, headline, the direction and titles you are targeting, what is unusual about you, your strengths, your gaps, and the kinds of role you have ruled out;
  • JSearch (OpenWeb Ninja), Adzuna, Jooble, Careerjet, Remotive, Remote OK, Workable and Apify (which runs the searches on Indeed, Naukri, Instahyre, CutShort, Foundit, Upwork and Google): the job titles and location you search for, to find postings. Careerjet also receives your IP address and browser type, which its API requires;
  • Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workable, SmartRecruiters and Recruitee public job feeds: company names, to read full job postings;
  • Mantiks, when you ask who to contact about a job: the company name, its website and the job title. This route is built but has not yet been used on any live lookup; the fallback route asks Apify to run public web searches for the same purpose;
  • Resend, only if you send a message through the contact page: the name, email address and message you type there, so it can be delivered to us as email.

Nothing in this list receives your password, your payment details, or any API key of yours.

When you use the app's credits or free searches, these requests go through our server using our API keys. If you add your own API keys, the app calls those providers directly from your browser and your keys are never uploaded to us; they stay in that browser.

We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Hosting and delivery

The site and its small API are served by Cloudflare, so every request to the app passes through their network and they process the ordinary technical details of it — your IP address, your browser type and the page requested. The database, accounts and the server that holds our API keys are hosted by Supabase, in their Sydney, Australia region (ap-southeast-2) — so your data is stored outside India, and transferred there when you use the app.

Two libraries are fetched by your browser from public CDNs the first time they are needed: the Supabase client from jsDelivr, and the PDF reader from cdnjs. Requesting a file tells that CDN your IP address and browser type. The PDF reader is the one that matters here: a PDF CV is read inside your browser and the file itself is never uploaded anywhere — though the text taken out of it is then sent to DeepSeek, as described above, to build your profile.

Without an account

Job Triage is also published as a single file you can run yourself. Used that way, with no server configured, your data stays in your browser's local storage, nothing is sent to us, and clearing your site data erases it permanently.

On this hosted site an account is required — the app asks you to sign in before it will show you anything. An earlier version of this policy said you could use it without signing in, which was true of the self-hosted file and not of this site.

Deleting and exporting your data

  • Delete everything in my account in the app erases every profile, job, CV and note, on every device, and leaves the account itself intact. It is immediate and cannot be undone.
  • Export CSV downloads your jobs at any time.
  • To close your account completely, including your email address and unused credits, email us from your registered address and we will do it within 7 days.

We keep purchase records only where the law requires us to for accounting.

Your rights and contact

We process your data with your consent, given by creating an account, and you can withdraw it at any time by deleting your account. You can access, correct, export and erase your data. For any privacy question or request, email banerjeesoumyadip1991@gmail.com.
