Components

Every primitive in the app, at its real values. New UI should be assembled from these before anything is invented — if a thing you need is not here, it is worth asking whether the design needs it or whether an existing piece was meant to do that job.

Buttons

One primary per context

Green is reserved for the single most likely next action in whatever you are looking at. Two greens side by side means the hierarchy has not been decided.

Segmented control

Filters, not navigation

A different shape from tabs on purpose: these change what a view shows, tabs change which view you are in. Used for the channel filter and the sort.

Chips and flags

Facts, not actions

Chips carry profile facts and board names. Flags are the scorer's verdicts, and only they get green or red — a flag is a judgement, a chip is a label.

Form

Input, label, hint

Inputs are mono, because most of what gets typed here is machine-ish — a location, a slug, a key. Hints sit under the control they explain and stay at 11px so they never compete with it.

Narrows the big portals to this place, and to anything remote. Blank searches worldwide.

Disclosure

Rare things, one press away

Native <details>. Used for the sidebar's rare half and for list sections you did not come for — below the bar, unscored, closed. Open state survives re-render.

The two axes

Fit and reachability

The core idea of the app, and the one thing never to collapse. Fit takes the channel colour; reachability is always grey. Each stated once, labelled, with the number beside its bar.

Empty and next

Say what to do, not what is missing

An empty state names the action that fills it. The banner above every tab is the single derived instruction — calm green when clear, red when something is overdue.
